Beginners Bread Baking Workshop

Make your own Bread after this extensive bread baking class which includes:

  • Perfect Soft Burger Buns topped with Sesame seeds
  • Sandwich Loaf (variations also explained)
  • Focaccia Bread (topped with olives & seasonings)
  • French Loaf (Baguette)
  • Crispy Lavash (Lebanese Chips)
  • Grissini (Soup Sticks)
  • Pita Bread

This class is a group activity and will involve baking in bulk in a hot room. We advise you wear breathable clothes and bring an apron along with you, to avoid being covered in flour. Please let us know in prior if you have any allergies.

Lunch is not provided for this class and you can either dine in with us at our Cafe

Good luck!
