About Us

Welcome to Tiers and Layers!

At Tiers and Layers, we believe that every celebration deserves something sweet and special. Born out of pure passion for home-baked goodness, our journey began with a single oven and a heart full of dreams.

We specialize in creating freshly baked mug cakes and desserts that not only taste divine but also bring a smile with every bite. Every product is handcrafted with love, using premium ingredients and no preservatives.

Whether it’s a birthday, anniversary, or just a craving — Tiers and Layers is your go-to place for delightful treats. We’ve served thousands of happy customers and continue to grow as a trusted brand among dessert lovers.

Thank you for being part of our sweet journey!
